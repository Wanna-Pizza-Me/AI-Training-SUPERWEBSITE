Legal AI training jobs

Lawyers, paralegals and contract professionals help AI labs build models that can reason about the law: drafting legal research tasks, grading model answers on case law and contracts, and catching invented citations. Many roles ask for a JD or bar admission, and legal work is some of the best-paid AI training there is. Right now 91 legal roles are open across Micro1, AfterQuery, Mercor, DataAnnotation, Turing and Meridial, and most of the hourly ones pay about $50–$400 an hour.
