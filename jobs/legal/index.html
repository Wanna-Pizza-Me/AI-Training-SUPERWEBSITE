Legal AI training jobs

Lawyers, paralegals and contract professionals help AI labs build models that can reason about the law: drafting legal research tasks, grading model answers on case law and contracts, and catching invented citations. Many roles ask for a JD or bar admission, and legal work is some of the best-paid AI training there is.
